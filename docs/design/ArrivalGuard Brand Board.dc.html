Brand and design system
Wayfinding for money in transit. A tuition payment leaves Lima, crosses currencies and intermediaries, and has to arrive at a university in Shenzhen. The interface borrows the language of transit signage so a family under pressure always knows where the money is and what comes next.

The consent seal

When Carlos approves, the document receives a square seal generated from the fingerprint of that exact approval. Change one detail of the plan and the fingerprint, and the seal, change with it.

Appears only on the approval document, the Approved station of the journey, Carlos's requests and the evidence timeline.
